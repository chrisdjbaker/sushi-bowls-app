Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy covers the Sushi Bowls app for iPhone and iPad (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. The developer runs no servers.

What stays on your device

Your restaurants, bowl prices, tax and tip settings, the meal in progress and your saved meal history are stored on your device only. This information never leaves your device unless your own device backup (such as iCloud Backup) includes it. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
